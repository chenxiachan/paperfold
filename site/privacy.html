Privacy policy: TextFold for the browser

Last updated October 28, 2026

TextFold for the browser is a button that sends the page you are reading to TextFold, the free program on your computer. This policy is about the extension. It is short because the extension has no server: it sends nothing over the internet.

What the extension reads, and when

Only when you click its button, and only in the tab you click it in:

  • the page as your browser shows it: its HTML, the text in its frames and shadow roots, its title and its address. This can include what the page shows only to you, such as your name when you are signed in;
  • the source of each formula the page's MathJax drew, asked of MathJax in the page;
  • the article in it, as two open-source reading views read it (Defuddle and Readability), run inside your browser.

It reads no other tab, no page you have not clicked it on, and nothing in the background. It does not read your browsing history, your cookies, your passwords, or what you type into forms.

Where it goes

The extension hands what it read to TextFold on your computer through the browser's native messaging, a channel between the browser and a program installed on the same computer. No network port is opened, and only TextFold receives it. The extension has no server, sends nothing over the network, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. It runs only the code it ships with.

What TextFold does with it

TextFold keeps the page in your library, a folder on your computer that you can open, move and delete. Its levels, from a one-screen map to every word, are written by the AI model you choose in TextFold's settings: a model that runs on your computer, or a model provider you connected with your own account or key. When TextFold is running, it starts writing the levels of a newly sent page at once, and the page's text goes to that model, under its provider's terms. With a model on your computer, the text stays on it.

TextFold itself asks GitHub for its newest version from time to time; nothing of yours is sent.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The developer of TextFold receives no data from the extension or from the app, and sells or shares none. The use of information received from the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing it

Delete a page in TextFold, or its folder in your library. Removing the extension stops it reading anything; removing TextFold removes the program it talks to, and your library stays yours to keep or delete.

Questions and changes

Ask in the project's issues on GitHub. If this policy changes, this page says so, with the date above. The extension's code is open, with TextFold's: github.com/chenxiachan/textfold.

隐私政策：TextFold 浏览器扩展

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日

TextFold 浏览器扩展是一个按钮，把你正在读的网页发给 TextFold，也就是你电脑上的免费软件。本政策只讲这个扩展。它很短，因为扩展没有服务器，不往互联网发送任何东西。

扩展读取什么，什么时候读取

只在你点按钮的时候，只读你点按钮的那个标签页：

  • 浏览器显示出来的网页：它的 HTML、框架和 shadow root 里的文字、标题和网址。其中可能包括网页只显示给你的内容，比如登录后的用户名；
  • 网页里 MathJax 排出的每个公式的源码，在网页里向 MathJax 询问得到；
  • 网页里的正文，由两个开源的阅读模式程序（Defuddle 和 Readability）在你的浏览器里读出。

它不读其他标签页，不读你没有点按钮的网页，也不在后台读取任何东西。它不读你的浏览记录、Cookie、密码，也不读你在表单里输入的内容。

内容去往哪里

扩展通过浏览器的原生消息（native messaging）把读到的内容交给你电脑上的 TextFold。这是浏览器和同一台电脑上已安装程序之间的通道，不开任何网络端口，只有 TextFold 能收到。扩展没有服务器，不通过网络发送任何东西，不含统计、广告或追踪代码，只运行它自带的代码。

TextFold 怎么处理

TextFold 把网页存进你的文献库，也就是你电脑上的一个文件夹，你可以打开、移动和删除。网页的各层（从一屏看完的地图到每一个字）由你在 TextFold 设置里选的 AI 模型来写：可以是在你电脑上运行的模型，也可以是你用自己的账号或密钥接入的模型服务商。TextFold 正在运行时，会马上开始为新发来的网页写各层，网页的文字会发给你选的模型，适用该服务商的条款。用本机模型时，文字留在你的电脑上。

TextFold 本身会不时向 GitHub 查询最新版本，不发送任何属于你的信息。

开发者收到什么

什么也收不到。TextFold 的开发者不从扩展或软件收集任何数据，也不出售或分享任何数据。扩展对所获信息的使用遵守 Chrome 网上应用店用户数据政策，包括其中的"有限使用"要求。

如何删除

在 TextFold 里删除网页，或者删除文献库里对应的文件夹。移除扩展后，它不再读取任何东西；卸载 TextFold 会移除扩展连接的程序，文献库仍归你保留或删除。

问题和变更

有问题请到 GitHub 上的 issues 提出。本政策如有变更，会在这一页说明，并更新上面的日期。扩展的代码和 TextFold 一起公开在 github.com/chenxiachan/textfold。
